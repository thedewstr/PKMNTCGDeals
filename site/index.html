FAQ

Is this automated or manual?

Our free server, PKMNTCGDeals, is a community-based effort to monitor stock. Our premium server, Gotta Ping 'Em All, uses monitoring services to track restocks of Pokémon, Magic, Gundam, and more.

Which stores do you cover?

We keep an eye on Pokémon Center, Walmart, Target, Amazon, Best Buy, GameStop, Costco, Barnes & Noble, and more.

How fast are alerts?

Normally we see updates a little before or right as they happen — that's the whole point of running monitors instead of refreshing pages yourself.

Where do the success images come from?

Straight from our community. When a member posts a win with a photo, our bot mirrors it and the gallery here picks it up automatically. See Privacy for details.
